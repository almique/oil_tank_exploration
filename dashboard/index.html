How this is measured

  • A floating roof sits on the oil. The tank wall casts a shadow outside the tank (length set by wall height and sun angle) and another onto the roof (length set by how far the roof sits below the rim). Fill = 1 − inner ÷ outer; sun angle and tank height cancel.
  • Fleet fill weights each floating-roof tank by its capacity. Fixed-roof tanks can't be measured this way and are excluded.
  • EIA utilization = weekly crude stocks ÷ working storage capacity (EIA storage capacity report, 2011–2024). Cushing compares one hub with itself; Gulf Coast sites only have region-wide EIA data, so their comparison is not a real test.
  • * EIA capacity data starts in March 2011, so earlier utilization uses the 2011 capacity.
  • Years with fewer than 30 measured tanks are shown hollow and left out of the statistics. NAIP flights near noon (short shadows) and 1 m imagery are less reliable.
  • Sources: NAIP on Microsoft Planetary Computer, OpenStreetMap tank outlines, EIA storage capacity, EIA weekly stocks.
